# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (user's choice). Deploy target: undecided.

## Users

Peers, collaborators, and people in Kaiden Bell's wider network who arrive from a link, a profile, or a conversation and want to know who Kaiden is and what Kaiden builds. The site is a personal-brand home base, not a hiring funnel or a client-acquisition page. Recruiters and potential clients may still visit, but the site does not need to be optimized for them.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site for Kaiden Bell that works across engineering, design, and creative technology. It gives the network one place to see who Kaiden is, look at real work, and get in touch. Success means a visitor leaves with an accurate, memorable sense of Kaiden and their work, and has an easy way to follow or reach Kaiden.

## Positioning

What only this site can offer is Kaiden: their face, name, and actual work, both code and design. It should read as one person who does both engineering and design, not as a generic developer template. The exact positioning line has not been written yet (see Brand Commitments).

## Operating Context

- Visitors typically come in from a shared link (social profiles, GitHub, messages) and look around briefly.
- Proof of work lives on GitHub (code) and in design case studies hosted on the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections at launch: Work, About, Contact.
- Journal is **dropped for now**. Add it back only once real writing exists, and never fill it with placeholder posts.
- Work covers two kinds of evidence: code repositories (link to GitHub) and design case studies.
- Undecided: deploy target, contact method (form vs. email vs. social links), and the specific projects and case studies to feature.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Kaiden Bell**.
- The mockup copy ("Engineer. Designer. Builder." and "Building at the intersection of technology, creativity, and people.") is **placeholder only**. The direction is right, but the final wording is still to be written. Don't treat it as confirmed voice.
- The disciplines shown in the mockups (Engineering, Design, Creative Tech, Product) are the same kind of placeholder. Confirm them before publishing.

## Product Principles

1. **The person comes first.** The visitor should come away knowing Kaiden, not the template.
2. **Show real work.** Every project shown links to real code or a real case study. Leave a gap empty rather than filling it with invented work.
3. **One person, two crafts.** Engineering and design are presented as one practice, not as two separate résumés.
4. **Ship small and honest.** Launch with the sections that have real content, and add more (like the Journal) only when they do too.
